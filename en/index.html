The system

Ventilated facade. ±2 mm per floor.

Survey and marking0%
  1. 01 · Survey

    Laser scan and bracket grid marking

    The structure is scanned and we get a deviation map of the concrete. Up to 30 mm of deviation is absorbed by the bracket adjustment — never by the panel. The bracket grid follows the wind-load calculation for each elevation.

    laser scan±30 mmSI 414
  2. 02 · Anchoring

    Bracket and anchor

    Drill, clean the hole, tighten to torque. Into concrete — a drive-in anchor; into block — a screw with a plastic plug. A fixed bracket carries dead load and wind; a sliding bracket carries wind only. On every floor a sample of anchors gets a pull-out test.

    A2/A4drive-in anchorpull-out test
  3. 03 · Insulation

    Mineral wool with no gaps

    80 mm mineral wool (thickness by SI 1045 calculation), 5 thermal discs per m², cut tight around every bracket. A gap in the insulation is a thermal bridge — so there are no gaps.

    80 mm5 pcs/m²SI 1045
  4. 04 · L-profile

    Vertical L-profile — one fixed point

    Black aluminium in lengths up to 6 m. Each length has one fixed point (round hole) and sliding points everywhere else (slots), with a 10 mm gap between lengths — so thermal expansion is never pushed into the panels.

    ≤ 6 m1 fixed point10 mm gap
  5. 05 · PR rail

    Horizontal PR rail on a laser line

    One horizontal rail per panel row, fixed to the verticals at the laser-marked height and levelled to ±1 mm per row. This is the track the panels will lock onto later.

    ±1 mm / rowlaser
  6. 06 · Check

    Frame handover before a single panel is hung

    Re-scan, a deviation log for every floor, a ventilation cavity from 30 mm (sized for the building height) and air openings of 50 cm² per metre of facade at the bottom and top. Hanging starts only after the site supervisor signs.

    ≥ 30 mm cavity≥ 50 cm²/m±2 mm / floor

02 · DEKTON

Installing Dekton, step by step

Not one screw is visible from the street. All the strength sits inside the panel, in an anchor that expands in a mushroom-shaped hole on its back.

Ø7 hole + Ø9 undercut in one passA4 sleeve expands in the undercutAluminium clip · screw to torqueClip locks onto the PR railAdjustment screw · panel heightJoint gauge · 6 mm
Panel layout0%
  1. 01 · Design

    Panel layout — an ID for every panel

    The layout is built from the scan. Every panel gets an ID printed on its back (e.g. B1 S-3) and an exact position on the elevation. 12 mm thickness, approved for ventilated facades under ETA 14/0413; panels up to 320×144 cm, about 30 kg/m².

    12 mm320×144 cm~30 kg/m²
  2. 02 · Cutting

    CNC cutting in the factory

    Computer-controlled cutting, bevelled edges, dimensions checked to ±0.5 mm. Every panel gets a QR code, so it can be traced from the factory to the floor.

    CNC±0.5 mmQR
  3. 03 · Drilling

    Undercut drilling in a single pass

    A KEIL system drill bores the Ø7 mm cylindrical hole and the ≥ Ø9 mm undercut in one motion; the depth is controlled by a guide. 4–6 anchor points per panel, depending on its size and the wind zone.

    Ø 7 mmundercut ≥ Ø 94–6 pts
  4. 04 · Anchor and clip

    Stainless anchor + aluminium clip

    The anchor sleeve (A4 stainless) is inserted together with the clip and the screw is tightened to a defined torque — the sleeve expands in the undercut with no pressure on the panel. A sample from every batch gets a pull-out test.

    A4 stainlesstorquesample pull test
  5. 05 · Hanging

    Vacuum lift, lock onto the PR rail

    The panel is lifted with vacuum cups and tilted to the facade; the clips on its back lock onto the PR rail. The two upper hangers with levelling screws carry the weight and set the height; the lower ones take wind only. The joint is 6 mm, checked with a gauge.

    vacuum lift2 adj. hangers6 mm joint
  6. 06 · Check

    A laser line for every row. Any panel can be replaced

    Deviation up to ±2 mm per floor, recorded in the log. No screws on the facade, and every panel can be removed and replaced on its own. Dekton: fire class A2-s1,d0, water absorption ≈0 (0.1 %), a written 10-year warranty.

    ±2 mmA2-s1,d0replaceable

03 · HPL

Installing HPL, step by step

HPL moves with humidity, up to 2.5 mm per metre. The whole installation is built around that fact.

EPDM tape on the T-profileSliding point · Ø10 holeFixed point · Ø5.1 holeØ5 rivet · 16 mm headSpacer nose · 0.3 mm playPanel expands — the rivet stays
Material and conditioning0%
  1. 01 · Material

    Compact HPL, 8 mm, 48 hours of conditioning

    Panels to EN 438-6 type EDF (exterior, flame retardant, class B-s1,d0), 8 mm thick (6–10 by project), UV-resistant surface. Panels are stored flat; as our practice, they are conditioned on site for 48 hours before drilling.

    EN 438-6 EDFB-s1,d08 mm
  2. 02 · Profiles

    T and L profiles with EPDM tape

    A T-profile at every vertical joint, L-profiles in the field of the panel, spacing up to 600 mm by wind calculation. Black EPDM tape on every profile: it seals the metal and hides it in the open joint.

    T / L profiles≤ 600 mmEPDM
  3. 03 · Drilling

    One fixed point, the rest sliding

    Drilled through a jig: a 5.1 mm hole for the fixed point in the centre of the panel, 10 mm holes for the sliding points everywhere else. Edge distance 20–80 mm — so the panel can move from the centre outwards.

    Ø 5.1 fixedØ 10 sliding20–80 mm edge
  4. 04 · Rivets

    Rivet with a spacer nose — holds, doesn't clamp

    Ø5 mm aluminium blind rivet with a 16 mm head in the panel colour. The rivet gun's spacer nose leaves about 0.3 mm of play, so the panel breathes. Work order: from the fixed point outwards.

    Ø 5 mmhead 16 mmspacer nose
  5. 05 · Joints

    8 mm open joint and a cavity that breathes

    8 mm open joints between panels, a 40 mm ventilation cavity (20 mm minimum), 50 cm² of air opening per metre at the bottom and top with insect mesh. The air flowing behind the panel is what keeps the wall dry.

    8 mm joint40 mm cavity≥ 50 cm²/m
  6. 06 · Option and check

    No rivets? There are two ways

    Structural adhesive on a primed profile, or undercut anchors as with Dekton. In every method, on site: rivet grid checked with a gauge, joint lines by laser, a deviation log for every floor. With the same system, on rivets colour-matched to the panel, we also install Dekton 8 mm.

    adhesive optionundercut optionlaser lines
