Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

AANA\CLADDING is committed to making this website accessible to people with disabilities, in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law (1998), the Accessibility of Services Regulations (2013) and Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What is in place

  • Semantic heading structure, full keyboard navigation and a visible focus indicator.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1; content scales to 200 % without loss.
  • Text alternatives for photos; 3D scenes and videos are decorative and carry no information that is not also in the text.
  • The “reduce motion” system setting disables animations.
  • Labelled form fields and clear messages.

Still in progress

A full SI 5568 audit is scheduled before the site goes live on its permanent domain. Some interactive 3D drawings may not be usable with screen readers; all of their information is duplicated in text.

Accessibility coordinator

If you have difficulty using the site, call +972 53-382-7159 or write to info@aana-cladding.co.il. We reply within two business days.
